**Short description:**
A simple spreadsheet that totals your freelance income and expenses by month and shows how much to set aside for taxes. No macros, no subscription.

**Description:**
Stop guessing how much of this month's income is really yours.

Enter each payment and cost once. The tracker does the rest:
- Monthly income after fees, expenses and profit for the whole year, with profit margin, payment count and average payment
- A tax set-aside amount based on a percentage YOU choose
- Optional monthly profit goal, highlighted when reached
- Expense categories you can edit; fees deducted at source (e.g. platform or payment fees) handled per income row
- Room for 500 income rows and 500 expense rows; reuse it every year by changing the year in Settings

What you get: a blank .xlsx, a filled-in sample .xlsx, and a one-page README.
Works in Excel, LibreOffice and Google Sheets. No macros.

Built with the help of AI tools. The formulas were checked by recalculating the workbook with sample data.

Please note: this is a bookkeeping aid, not tax, legal or accounting advice. The set-aside rate is a planning figure you choose.
